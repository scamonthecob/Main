COB-001 · IMJAOBAO’S ACCOUNT

A reported unpaid $10 raffle

Claimant account + supplied message text

ImJaoBao reports payment remains outstanding as of 8 October 2026. Earlier prizes were paid. Winning reference: VOD 2889810819 · 04:38:45 on 2 October 2026. Streamlabs announced: “@ImJaoBao has won a $10 Prize Money!”

01

A cash prize was announced

I won a $10 raffle and was told to message CamOnTheCob to claim it. In the giveaway announcements I heard, entry required following the channel and being present at the drawing. I did not hear a restriction on previous winners or a minimum level of chat activity.

Account from ImJaoBao. Check the supplied VOD and the rules in force at the time.

02

Cash became a loyalty discussion

When I asked for cash, the conversation repeatedly returned to my earlier wins, gifted subscriptions and how much I typed in chat. I was encouraged to accept subs or gift the prize back instead.

“I just don’t like it when sometimes it starts to feel like people are just using me, know what I mean?”CamOnTheCob, in the Whisper text supplied by ImJaoBao
03

A promise to pay

“I will send the prize money again to you”CamOnTheCob, in the supplied exchange about this raffle

He also offered to gift the prize out in my honor. I chose the $10 payment and requested it again. He has paid my earlier prizes; this complaint concerns the latest $10.

04

Exclusion after the win

“My rules state the giveaways are for community members, and you are no longer a community member”CamOnTheCob, in the latest supplied Whisper

My payment remains outstanding as of 8 October 2026. This message does not explicitly say that the prize has been cancelled. The question is whether an exclusion imposed after my win is being used to withhold it.

The claimant reports submitting a VOD report to Twitch and receiving no response as of 8 October 2026. No enforcement finding is known.

The quotes above come from message text supplied by ImJaoBao. Original screenshots have not been attached to this page, and relative message dates still need confirmation.
