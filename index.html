WHY THIS RECORD IS NECESSARY

A cash promise should survive the stream.

The giveaway practices challenged here concern how cash prizes are advertised, how winners are notified and what happens when they try to claim. The maintainer reports the following recurring behavior on CamOnTheCob’s streams.

01 · THE OFFER

Open giveaways. Later conditions.

Races and raffles are described as free, with no subscription required. The reported entry conditions are following the channel and being present at the drawing. Previous wins and “community” participation have then been raised when a winner requested cash.

Eligibility should be clear before entry.

02 · THE CLAIM

Cash advertised. Subs encouraged.

Winners can be asked to accept subscriptions instead of money, or gift their winnings back. Comments about generosity, earlier prizes and whether a viewer is “using” the streamer can make claiming the advertised cash feel like a loyalty test.

Supporting a stream should remain a choice.

03 · THE RECORD

A brief announcement can be missed.

Cash winners are reportedly announced briefly and told to message the streamer or he will “forget.” The maintainer reports inconsistent visible tracking of those winners, making a missed announcement difficult to recover later.

A missed announcement should not erase a win.

The concern is that cash prizes attract participation, while claiming them can bring pressure or conditions that viewers did not hear when they entered. This archive keeps the announcement, the promised prize and subsequent updates together, so those concerns can be checked against a record.

RECOVER THE HISTORY

Missed winners still belong here.

The goal is to add all identifiable winners from previous broadcasts and keep recording future races and raffles. A winner can be listed even if they missed the announcement, never contacted the streamer, received payment or have no complaint.

The number of missed or unpaid prizes is currently unknown. An unclaimed prize and a refused payment are different situations. Record the winner first, then add the claim and payment information when it becomes available.

The requested outcome: honor valid advertised prizes, disclose entry rules clearly and keep reliable winner records.

HOW THE ARCHIVE GROWS

An announcement. A source. A dated update.

Add past winners

Review the broadcast, record the public username, prize and date, and link the VOD at the winner announcement. Add relevant rules and context. Leave missing details unconfirmed.

Record future winners

New announcements can be added to the same archive. A future chat collector will help find candidates; each candidate needs review. Spoken or on-screen announcements may still require manual checks.

Keep the outcome current

Track payment separately from the announcement. Add winner confirmations, supporting evidence and corrections with dates. Keep paid records alongside disputed and unknown records.

The archive is being assembled. The automatic collector is planned and is not running yet.
