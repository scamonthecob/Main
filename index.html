WHY THIS PAGE EXISTS

CamOnTheCob is a con artist.

That is the maintainer’s conclusion, and it is why this site exists. CamOnTheCob’s stream is treated here as a show built to pull in viewers with cash prizes and then convert that attention into subscriptions. The advertised money is the bait. The archive below exists so winners and promises are harder to erase after the live stream ends.

Near-impossible maps. Big money as bait.

On Marbles on Stream he runs maps with almost no realistic chance of winning, while offering a large cash prize to attract people into the channel.

Easier maps. Quiet winners. “I’ll forget.”

He also runs easier maps with smaller prizes. Winners are told to contact him or he will “forget.” If you are not in his private circle of people who gift him subs, the winner name is barely announced.

End-of-stream raffles keep new viewers watching.

A raffle at the end of the stream is used to hold new viewers until close. The winner is still poorly announced, the name is not kept, and he can “forget” to contact them.

Contact him and the prize becomes a sub pitch.

Even when a winner does message him, he pressures them to take gifted subs instead of cash. Refuse that swap too many times and, on this account, he simply does not pay.

Skin winners are saved. Cash winners are not.

He also offers marble-skin prizes. Those winner names he actually records. Cash-prize names are the ones that go missing. The maintainer’s view is that this is on purpose, so cash does not have to be paid.

If he refuses to pay a prize you won, report him on Twitch.

File one detailed report with the VOD, timestamp, prize and what happened when you tried to claim. Do not raid chat, mass-report, or contact other viewers on this site’s behalf.

WHAT THE ARCHIVE IS FOR

A cash promise should survive the stream.

Cash is advertised as free to enter. Follow the channel, be there for the drawing. Previous wins and “community” participation get raised later, when someone asks to be paid. This archive keeps the announcement, the promised prize and later updates together so that pattern can be checked against a record instead of disappearing into chat.

The requested outcome remains the same: honor valid advertised prizes, say the rules before people enter, and keep a reliable winner list. Until that happens, the names belong here.

RECOVER THE HISTORY

Missed winners still belong here.

The goal is to add all identifiable winners from previous broadcasts and keep recording future races and raffles. A winner can be listed even if they missed the announcement, never contacted the streamer, received payment or have no complaint.

The number of missed or unpaid prizes is currently unknown. An unclaimed prize and a refused payment are different situations. Record the winner first, then add the claim and payment information when it becomes available.

The requested outcome: honor valid advertised prizes, disclose entry rules clearly and keep reliable winner records.

HOW THE ARCHIVE GROWS

An announcement. A source. A dated update.

Add past winners

Review the broadcast, record the public username, prize and date, and link the VOD at the winner announcement. Add relevant rules and context. Leave missing details unconfirmed.

Record future winners

New announcements can be added to the same archive. A future chat collector will help find candidates; each candidate needs review. Spoken or on-screen announcements may still require manual checks.

Keep the outcome current

Track payment separately from the announcement. Add winner confirmations, supporting evidence and corrections with dates. Keep paid records alongside disputed and unknown records.

The archive is being assembled from raffles first. Fall Bash (three streams, no raffles) and the 6 Years Celebration VOD are queued for later. The automatic collector is not running yet.
