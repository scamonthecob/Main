IMJAOBAO’S ACCOUNT

Why this complaint exists

Claimant account + supplied message text
01

A cash prize was announced

I won a $10 raffle and was told to message CamOnTheCob to claim it. In the giveaway announcements I heard, entry required following the channel and being present at the drawing. I did not hear a restriction on previous winners or a minimum level of chat activity.

Account from ImJaoBao. Check the supplied VOD and the rules in force at the time.

02

Cash became a loyalty discussion

When I asked for cash, the conversation repeatedly returned to my earlier wins, gifted subscriptions and how much I typed in chat. I was encouraged to accept subs or gift the prize back instead.

“I just don’t like it when sometimes it starts to feel like people are just using me, know what I mean?”CamOnTheCob, in the Whisper text supplied by ImJaoBao
03

A promise to pay

“I will send the prize money again to you”CamOnTheCob, in the supplied exchange about this raffle

He also offered to gift the prize out in my honor. I chose the $10 payment and requested it again. He has paid my earlier prizes; this complaint concerns the latest $10.

04

Exclusion after the win

“My rules state the giveaways are for community members, and you are no longer a community member”CamOnTheCob, in the latest supplied Whisper

My payment remains outstanding as of 8 October 2026. This message does not explicitly say that the prize has been cancelled. The question is whether an exclusion imposed after my win is being used to withhold it.

The quotes above come from message text supplied by ImJaoBao. Original screenshots have not been attached to this page, and relative message dates still need confirmation.

A SECOND CONCERN

Can winners tell they won?

ImJaoBao reports brief cash-winner announcements followed by instructions to send a message or the streamer will “forget.” He also reports seeing names retained for familiar viewers and skin prizes, without consistent visible tracking of other cash winners.

This raises a question about how winners are notified and how unpaid prizes are tracked. The number of missed or unpaid prizes is unknown. This page does not treat every unclaimed prize as unpaid or assume that other winners had the same experience.

Other observations awaiting evidence

The viewer also described very difficult prize maps, lengthy talking segments under the Marbles on Stream category, viewers mocked after asking for a race to start, and sexual discussions or frequent profanity without the corresponding content labels.

No specific clips, verified map odds or label records have been supplied for these observations. They are not listed as established policy violations or proof of fraud.

HOW RECORDS ARE HANDLED

Evidence first. Corrections welcome.

Keep a source for each win

Record the public username, date, prize, VOD or clip, timestamp and rules announced before entry. Unknown details stay unknown. Future chat detections must be reviewed before they are published.

Track payment separately

Payment status needs information from the winner or payment evidence. If a payment is confirmed, update the record and retain the correction date. Do not infer nonpayment from chat silence.

Ask for a fair resolution

The requested outcome is payment of valid outstanding prizes, clear eligibility rules, and consistent winner notification. The viewer says he submitted reports to Twitch and has received no response as of 8 October 2026. No enforcement finding has been provided.

Correction or response? Send the record ID and supporting evidence to the maintainer through this project’s GitHub repository once published. CamOnTheCob’s response or evidence of payment can be recorded alongside the complaint.

Twitch’s Terms of Service place responsibility for giveaway rules, administration and issuing prizes on the promoter. That responsibility does not itself establish a violation in this case.
